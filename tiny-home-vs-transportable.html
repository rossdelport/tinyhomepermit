Tiny home vs transportable home

Short answer

A tiny home on wheels is legally a caravan: no building permit, but you can only live in it for up to 24 months at a time with council approval. A transportable home on footings is a building: it needs a building permit and must meet the building code, but once approved you can live in it permanently.

Last updated 14 November 2026

The key difference: wheels

A tiny home on wheels that stays towable is treated as a caravan in WA. A transportable home is built off-site and then placed on footings or stumps on your land, so it's a building. That one difference changes almost every rule.

No shortcut for prefab

WA's building regulator and Consumer Protection have warned that there are no special exemptions for prefabricated or modular buildings. Claims that a kit or transportable home is "permit-free" may be misleading. Electrical and plumbing work must be done by licensed trades.

Buying tips from Consumer Protection

  • Pay a small deposit, with progress payments tied to manufacturing, delivery and installation
  • Ask for evidence of progress before each payment
  • Visit the factory or site if you can

Which suits you?

  • Tiny home on wheels if you need to move it, or only want a temporary home while you build.
  • Transportable home if you want a permanent home that you can live in for good once it's approved, often as a granny flat.

Questions

Common questions

Does a transportable home need a building permit in WA?

Yes. Once it's on footings or stumps it's a building, and there are no special exemptions for prefabricated or modular buildings.

Is a tiny home on wheels a transportable home?

No. A tiny home on wheels that stays towable is a caravan. A transportable home is placed on footings and becomes a building.

Can I live permanently in a transportable home?

Yes, once it's approved as a house or granny flat. A tiny home on wheels can't be lived in permanently outside a caravan park under current rules.

Who can install a transportable home?

If it's a habitable building over $20,000 that needs a permit, a registered builder or an approved owner-builder.
