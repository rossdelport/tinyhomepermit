Question answer

Can I put a tiny home in my backyard in Perth?

Short answer

Yes. A fixed tiny home can be approved as a granny flat up to 70 m², with a building permit, and lived in permanently. A tiny home on wheels is a caravan: up to 5 nights in 28 days with the owner's OK, or up to 24 months with council approval.

Last updated 16 November 2026

It depends on the wheels

In WA, a tiny home is either a caravan (on wheels and built to be towed) or a building (no wheels). They follow different laws. Not sure which yours is? Use the caravan or building checker.

Tiny home on wheels in the backyard

Under the state's caravan regulations:

  • Someone can stay in it for up to 5 nights in any 28 days with the landowner's OK.
  • For longer, your council can approve up to 24 months in a row. It can ask for power, water, sewage and cooking facilities. If it hasn't decided within 63 days, the application is treated as refused.
  • After 24 months you need to apply again. It can't be lived in permanently outside a caravan park.
  • Only one caravan can be used for camping on a lot at a time unless approved.
  • It has to keep its wheels and be able to be moved within 24 hours of disconnecting services.

Fixed tiny home in the backyard

Take the wheels off (or build it on footings) and it's a building. Behind an existing house, it's usually a granny flat (ancillary dwelling):

  • Up to 70 m², one per lot, with no minimum lot size.
  • No planning approval if it meets all the standard R-Codes rules (setbacks, site cover, height, privacy, parking).
  • A building permit and full National Construction Code compliance.
  • It can be lived in by family, friends or tenants.

Some councils add their own rules. For example, in the Rockingham city centre a granny flat must sit behind the main house and match its materials. Councils vary, so check yours, or read our City of Rockingham tiny home guide.

Which is right for you?

If you want it there for good, a fixed tiny home approved as a granny flat is the only permanent option. A tiny home on wheels suits a stay of up to 24 months at a time, such as while you build a house. See living in a caravan on your own land for more on the 24-month approval.

Questions

Common questions

Can I put a tiny home in my backyard in Perth?

Yes. A fixed tiny home can be approved as a granny flat up to 70 m² with a building permit. A tiny home on wheels can stay up to 5 nights in 28 days, or up to 24 months with council approval.

Can someone live permanently in a tiny home on wheels in my backyard?

No. A tiny home on wheels is a caravan, and caravans can't be lived in permanently outside a caravan park. Council can approve up to 24 months at a time.

Do I need a building permit for a tiny home in my backyard?

A fixed tiny home always needs one. A genuine towable tiny home on wheels doesn't, because it's a caravan.

Can I rent out a tiny home in my backyard?

A fixed tiny home approved as a granny flat can be rented to anyone. Ask your council about renting a tiny home on wheels, as it needs their approval to be lived in long term.
