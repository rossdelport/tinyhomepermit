Is my tiny home a caravan or a building?

Short answer

If your tiny home is on wheels and built to be towed, it's a caravan: no building permit, but you can only live in it for up to 24 months at a time with council approval. Without wheels, it's a building that needs a building permit. Answer three questions below.

Last updated 3 November 2026

Caravan or building?

Up to three quick questions.

Why it matters

In WA, a tiny home is either a caravan or a building. It can't be both. That one question decides which laws apply, whether you need a building permit and how long you can live in it.

Caravan (on wheels, towable)Building (no wheels)
Building permitNot neededNeeded
Building code (NCC)Doesn't applyMust comply
Planning approvalGenerally not neededNot needed if it meets the R-Codes; otherwise yes
How long you can live in it5 nights (owner's OK), up to 24 months with council approvalPermanently, once approved

What counts as a vehicle

The state's tiny house fact sheet says a vehicle must be a means of transport, not just capable of being moved. A tribunal case in 2018 (Henville and City of Armadale) found that vehicles and buildings are mutually exclusive. If you take the wheels off a caravan, it becomes a building and has to meet building rules.

Park homes

A park home is a home on wheels that doesn't need a vehicle licence because it's too big to be towed on the road. Park homes can only be lived in inside a licensed caravan park.

Sources

  • Tiny houses fact sheet, December 2024 (WA Planning Commission)
  • Temporary accommodation and camping outside of caravan parks, updated June 2026 (WA Government)

Questions

Common questions

Is a tiny home on wheels a caravan in WA?

Yes, if it's a genuine vehicle fitted for living in. The state says a vehicle must be a means of transport, not just able to be moved.

Can a tiny home be both a caravan and a building?

No. A 2018 tribunal case (Henville and City of Armadale) found that vehicles and buildings are mutually exclusive.

What happens if I take the wheels off my tiny home?

It stops being a caravan and becomes a building. It then needs a building permit and has to meet the National Construction Code.

Can I live in a tiny home on wheels permanently in WA?

Not outside a caravan park. You can live in it for up to 24 months at a time with owner and council approval, then apply again.
