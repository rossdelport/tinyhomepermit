How long can I live in a tiny home on wheels?

Short answer

In WA you can stay up to 5 nights in any 28 days without approval. For longer, your council can approve up to 24 consecutive months at a time. Only one can be lived in per lot without extra approval. Answer up to four questions below.

Last updated 10 November 2026

How long can I stay?

Up to four quick questions.

The time limits at a glance

How longWhat you need
Up to 5 nights in any 28 daysNothing, on land you own or have a legal right to occupy
More than 5 nights, up to 24 consecutive monthsWritten approval from your council (or the Minister if the council refuses)
More than one caravan on the lotCouncil approval up to 3 months; Minister beyond that
PermanentlyTake it off its wheels and get a building permit

These limits come from the state's Caravan Parks and Camping Grounds Regulations. Councils can add conditions, and some also need planning approval for a tiny home on wheels, so councils vary.

Rules while you're there

  • The tiny home must keep its wheels on and be able to be moved within 24 hours of its services being disconnected. The fine is up to $5,000.
  • It must be kept so it isn't a hazard to safety or health. The fine is also up to $5,000.
  • A council can cancel its approval if a condition is broken, but must give you 35 days' notice to respond first.

Not sure if yours counts as a caravan? Try the caravan or building checker. Read more about tiny home on wheels rules or living in a caravan on your own land.

Sources

  • Caravan Parks and Camping Grounds Regulations 1997, regs 11 to 15 (WA legislation)
  • Temporary accommodation and camping outside of caravan parks, updated June 2026 (WA Government)

Questions

Common questions

How long can I stay in a tiny home on wheels without approval in WA?

Up to 5 nights in any 28 days, on land you own or have a legal right to occupy.

How long can a council approve a tiny home on wheels for?

Up to 24 consecutive months. You can apply again after that.

What if the council doesn't answer my application?

If it doesn't approve within 63 days, the application is treated as refused. You can then apply to the Minister within 35 days.

Can I have two tiny homes on wheels on my lot?

Only with written approval. The council can approve more than one caravan for up to 3 months; the Minister for longer. The fine is up to $2,000.

Do I have to keep the wheels on?

Yes. A caravan must keep its wheels and be able to be moved within 24 hours of services being disconnected. The fine is up to $5,000.
