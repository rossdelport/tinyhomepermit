City of Wanneroo tiny home rules

Short answer

The City of Wanneroo only approves living in a tiny home on wheels for up to 3 months in any 12 months, for family members of the owner, while the owner lives in the house. On rural-type land you can live on site for 12 months while building a house. Long term, a tiny home usually needs approval as a building.

Last updated 19 November 2026

Tiny homes on wheels: Wanneroo is strict

Under WA law, councils can approve someone living in a caravan or tiny home on wheels on private land for up to 24 months. The City of Wanneroo sets much tighter limits in its Temporary Camping Guideline:

RuleCity of Wanneroo
How longUp to 3 months in any 12 months
Who can stayOnly family members of the property owner
OwnerMust live in the house on the property at the same time
TypeA registered, roadworthy caravan or vehicle with its own toilet, shower and cooking
Vacant residential landUsually not approved

Conditions

  • Connected to drinking water.
  • Toilet waste emptied into a toilet or sewer, and grey water into sewer or a proper system, safely.
  • A smoke alarm, fire blanket and dry powder fire extinguisher inside.
  • Parked on a hard surface inside your boundaries, meeting the City's setbacks.
  • No generators for power, and noise rules apply.

The City can cancel approval if the rules aren't followed. Apply to the City's Health Services using the online temporary camping licence form.

Living in it while you build

On Rural, Rural Resource, Rural Residential and Landscape Enhancement land, the owner and close family can live on site for 12 months while building a house. You need the house's approvals first, the slab must be down, and wastewater approval must be in place. The time can be extended if the house is nearly finished.

Want to stay longer?

For permanent living, a tiny home usually has to be approved as a building, such as a granny flat, with a building permit. In a case reported by the ABC in October 2025, the City treated a tiny home in a front yard as an ancillary dwelling (granny flat) rather than a tiny home on wheels. Ask the City early. Councils vary a lot on tiny homes.

Read the WA tiny home on wheels rules, about living in a caravan on your own land, or the fixed tiny home permit.

Questions

Common questions

Can I live in a tiny home on wheels in the City of Wanneroo?

Only short term. Wanneroo approves up to 3 months in any 12 months, for family members of the owner, while the owner lives in the house.

Can I rent out a tiny home on wheels in Wanneroo?

No. Wanneroo's temporary camping approval is only for family members of the property owner.

Can I live in a caravan while building a house in Wanneroo?

On rural-type zones, yes, for 12 months once the house is approved and the slab is down. It can be extended if the house is nearly finished.

How do I keep a tiny home in Wanneroo long term?

You'd usually need it approved as a building, such as a granny flat, with a building permit. Ask the City first.
