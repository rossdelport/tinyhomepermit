Council guide

Shire of Serpentine-Jarrahdale tiny home rules

Short answer

In Serpentine-Jarrahdale, a tiny home on wheels used for more than 5 days in any 28 needs development approval, and can be approved for up to 24 months. A fixed tiny home can be up to 100 m² as a house or granny flat, one per lot, with a building permit.

Last updated 5 November 2026

Tiny homes on wheels

The Shire treats a tiny home on wheels that can be towed as a caravan (or a park home). On private land:

  • Using it for more than 5 days in any 28-day period needs development approval as a temporary land use under the Shire's Local Planning Scheme No. 3.
  • Council can approve it for up to 24 months in a row under the state's caravan regulations.
  • If you're waiting to build a house on the lot, the Shire's Local Planning Policy 4.5 (Short Term and Temporary Accommodation) may support a tiny home on wheels in the meantime.

Approvals come with conditions: the tiny home must be kept safe and healthy, and the land must be suitable, including access to services.

Tiny homes not on wheels

A fixed tiny home is assessed as a single house (if it's the only home on the lot) or an ancillary dwelling (if there's already a house). The Shire's scheme allows up to 100 m², more than the 70 m² in the standard R-Codes. Only one fixed tiny home can be approved per lot.

It must meet the building code and the Shire's health local laws, including:

  • a kitchen, bathroom and laundry
  • ceilings at least 2.4 m in living spaces and 2.1 m in other spaces
  • structural certification from an engineer
  • connection to sewer, drinking water and electricity (including hot water), plus energy efficiency and sanitation standards
  • a BAL report and Bushfire Management Statement if the lot is bushfire-prone

The Shire recommends using a private building surveyor to certify the building permit application.

Common questions

Can I live in a tiny home on wheels in Serpentine-Jarrahdale?

Yes, with approval. More than 5 days in any 28-day period needs development approval, and the Shire can approve it for up to 24 months in a row.

How big can a fixed tiny home be in Serpentine-Jarrahdale?

Up to 100 m² under the Shire's Local Planning Scheme No. 3. Only one can be approved per lot.

Can I live in a tiny home while building my house in Serpentine-Jarrahdale?

The Shire's Local Planning Policy 4.5 may support a tiny home on wheels while you wait to build a house on the lot.

What does a fixed tiny home need in Serpentine-Jarrahdale?

A kitchen, bathroom and laundry, 2.4 m ceilings in living spaces, engineer's certification, connection to sewer, water and power, and a BAL report if bushfire-prone.
