Shire of Bridgetown-Greenbushes tiny home rules

Short answer

In the Shire of Bridgetown-Greenbushes, a tiny home on wheels is a caravan. You can stay up to 5 nights with the owner's OK, or up to 24 months with Shire approval, and only one per lot. A fixed tiny home is a building and needs a building permit.

Last updated 28 November 2026

Caravan or building?

The Shire of Bridgetown-Greenbushes says a "tiny home" isn't its own legal category. It must be one or the other, never both:

If your tiny home is…It's treated as…Approvals usually needed
On wheels and can be towedA caravanPublic health
Fixed and permanentA buildingPlanning, building and public health
Changed, such as wheels taken offA buildingPlanning, building and public health

Being on wheels alone doesn't make it a caravan. It must really be a vehicle used for transport. Check yours with the caravan or building checker.

Living in a tiny home on wheels

  • Up to 5 nights: with the property owner's OK.
  • Up to 24 months: with Shire approval. Conditions may apply.
  • Permanently: no. Caravans can't be lived in permanently outside a licensed caravan park.
  • One per lot: the Shire won't approve more than one caravan for living in on a single lot.
  • While you build a house: often allowed for a time, with public health approval for water, wastewater and power.

A caravan doesn't need a building permit, but a patio or other structure built next to it may. Plan your dates with the stay calculator.

Fixed tiny homes

A fixed tiny home is a building. It must meet the National Construction Code and get a building permit, and it may need development approval depending on its use and the zoning. There's no minimum size, but it must be fit to live in. A ready-made tiny home must be approved before it's installed. See fixed tiny home permits.

It may be approved as a house, a granny flat (if there's already an approved house on the lot), or short-term rental accommodation, which always needs development approval.

Water, wastewater and power

Every place people live, caravan or building, needs approved wastewater disposal and a safe water supply. A safe power supply is usually needed too. Tents aren't supported for any kind of accommodation.

Questions

Common questions

Can I live in a tiny home on wheels in Bridgetown?

For up to 5 nights with the owner's OK, or up to 24 months with Shire approval. Living in one permanently outside a caravan park isn't allowed.

Does a tiny home need a building permit in Bridgetown-Greenbushes?

Only if it's treated as a building, for example fixed in place or with the wheels taken off. A genuine caravan doesn't, but a patio next to it may.

How many tiny homes on wheels can I have on one lot in Bridgetown?

One. The Shire says it won't approve more than one caravan for living in on a single lot.

Can I live in a caravan while I build in Bridgetown?

Usually yes, for a time, if you have public health approval for water, wastewater and power.
