Council guide

City of Rockingham tiny home rules

Short answer

In Rockingham, a towable tiny home on wheels is a caravan: you can stay 5 nights in 28 days with the owner's OK, or up to 24 months with council approval. A fixed tiny home is a building: usually a granny flat up to 70 m² that always needs a building permit.

Last updated 15 November 2026

First: wheels or no wheels?

In WA, a tiny home on wheels that's built to be towed is a caravan. A tiny home without wheels is a building. The rules are completely different, so work out which yours is first with the caravan or building checker.

Tiny home on wheels in Rockingham

The City of Rockingham handles approvals to camp in or occupy a caravan on land that isn't a caravan park. The state rules it applies are:

How longWhat you need
Up to 5 nights in any 28 daysThe landowner's OK
Up to 24 months in a rowCouncil approval
PermanentlyNot allowed outside a caravan park

Other state rules to know:

  • Council can set conditions, such as being safe and healthy and having access to power, water and sewage.
  • If council hasn't decided within 63 days, the application is treated as refused. You can appeal to the Minister within 35 days.
  • Only one caravan can be used for camping on a lot at a time, unless approved.
  • It must keep its wheels and be able to be moved within 24 hours of disconnecting services.

Fixed tiny home in Rockingham

Without wheels, a tiny home is a building. On a residential lot it's either the main house or a granny flat (ancillary dwelling):

  • As a granny flat: up to 70 m², one per lot, on any lot size. It usually needs no planning approval if it meets the standard R-Codes rules.
  • As the main house: the City says single houses on residential land don't need development approval.
  • Special Residential and Special Rural lots need town planning consent for any development.
  • A building permit is always needed, and it must meet the National Construction Code.

In the Rockingham Strategic Centre (city centre plan area), a granny flat must sit behind the main house and suit its materials and colours. The plan restricts dongas and sea containers that haven't been properly converted.

Council fees for a fixed tiny home

From the City's fee sheet (updated 7 July 2026): a BA2 uncertified permit is 0.32% of the cost (minimum $121), a BA1 certified permit 0.19% (minimum $121), plus the building services levy of 0.137% (minimum $61.65). For a $60,000 tiny home with a BA2, that's $192 + $82.20 = $274.20.

See living in a caravan on your own land and tiny home approval cost for more. Call the City on 9528 0333 to talk about your site.

Questions

Common questions

Can I live in a tiny home on wheels in Rockingham?

For up to 5 nights in any 28 days with the landowner's OK. For longer, up to 24 months at a time, you need approval from the City of Rockingham.

Can I put a fixed tiny home in my backyard in Rockingham?

Yes, as a granny flat up to 70 m², one per lot. It needs a building permit and usually no planning approval if it meets the R-Codes.

Do I need a building permit for a tiny home in Rockingham?

A fixed tiny home always needs one. A genuine towable tiny home on wheels is a caravan and doesn't need a building permit.

How long does Rockingham have to decide a caravan application?

Under the state regulations, if council hasn't decided within 63 days, the application is treated as refused, and you can appeal to the Minister.
