How to renew your tiny home approval after 24 months

Short answer

In WA, a council can approve a tiny home on wheels on private land for up to 24 months. It doesn't renew itself. Before it ends, re-apply to your council with the owner's OK again. If the council refuses, the Minister for Local Government can consider it.

Last updated 27 November 2026

How the 24-month approval works

Under WA's Caravan Parks and Camping Grounds Regulations, living in a caravan or tiny home on wheels on private land works like this:

How longWho needs to approve
Up to 5 nightsThe property owner
Up to 24 monthsThe property owner and the local council
After 24 months (re-applying)The property owner and the local council again

So there's no automatic extension. When your approval is close to ending, you re-apply. Work out your dates with the stay calculator.

Steps to re-apply

  1. Check your end date on your current approval, and start early so there's no gap.
  2. Get the owner's written OK again, if you're not the owner.
  3. Lodge a new application with your council on its "approval to camp" form.
  4. Show the conditions are still met. Councils can attach health and safety conditions, such as enough power, water, sewage and cooking facilities.
  5. Tell the council about changes. In the Shire of Denmark, for example, a different tiny home needs a new application, while the same one leaving and coming back keeps its approval.

If the council says no

The Minister for Local Government can consider a camping approval if a council refuses or revokes one. An approval given by the Minister can't be revoked by the council.

Want to stay for good?

Living permanently is a different path. A tiny home built and fixed to the ground to meet the building code can get a building permit, like any small home. See fixed tiny home permits and tiny home on wheels rules.

Councils vary, so check your own council's form and conditions.

Questions

Common questions

What happens when my 24-month tiny home approval ends in WA?

You re-apply. The State's fact sheet says re-applying after 24 months needs approval from the property owner and the local government again.

Is a tiny home approval renewed automatically?

No. You need to lodge a new application with your council before the current approval ends.

What if my council refuses my tiny home application?

The Minister for Local Government can consider a camping approval if a council refuses or revokes one, and the council can't revoke the Minister's approval.

Do I need a new approval if I swap tiny homes?

Often yes. In the Shire of Denmark, a different tiny home needs a new application, while the same one coming back keeps its approval.
