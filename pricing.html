Prices

Our prices, upfront

Most permit services won't tell you a price until you call, and then council fees are added on top. Our prices are the total, with council fees included, so you know the cost before you start.

Tiny home on wheels$1,450Council application for living in it. Council fees included.Tiny home on a slab$2,450Plus council fees, which depend on the build value. We show you the exact amount before you pay.BAL (bushfire) assessment$595Done by an accredited assessor. $495 when added to another job.

Always included

  • Site plan and drawings
  • Application forms and owner consent
  • Lodging with your council
  • Council fees and levies (unless a price says otherwise)
  • Answering council's questions until your permit is issued

Only if your job needs it

  • Planning approval, if your council asks for it
  • Custom engineering if your kit doesn't come with it (+$450)
  • Heritage areas, or fixing something that already exists
  • Travel outside the Perth metro area

We tell you about any of these before you pay anything. Council fees are included for jobs worth up to $50,000.
