Shire of Augusta Margaret River tiny home rules

Short answer

The Shire of Augusta Margaret River can approve living in a tiny home on wheels for up to 24 months, on a lot that already has a house. It must be up to 50 m², lived in permanently (not a short-term rental), and at BAL-29 or lower in bushfire areas.

Last updated 22 November 2026

What the Shire allows

Since the state's caravan and camping rules changed on 1 September 2024, the Shire of Augusta Margaret River can approve:

  • Living in a tiny home on wheels on private land, for up to 24 months at a time.
  • Camping in one caravan while you build a house, once the house has a building permit, on R5 or lower residential lots and rural-type zones.

What counts as a tiny home on wheels

For the Shire, a tiny home on wheels is up to 50 m², built on a trailer, looks and works like a house, and is lived in permanently. It is not a short-term rental, and not a bus, van, tent or yurt.

Where it can go

  • On a lot that already has a house, where a granny flat could otherwise be approved under the R-Codes or the zone rules.
  • In a bushfire-prone area, only where the spot is rated BAL-29 or lower.
  • Inside any approved building envelope, and grouped with the existing buildings, sharing the driveway, water and waste systems where possible.

A toilet, shower and laundry must be available that meet the building code and health rules. An on-site wastewater system needs a Shire "permit to use".

After you apply

If the Shire hasn't approved it within 63 days, it's taken as refused. If it refuses, you can apply to the Minister for Local Government. You also need the Minister's approval for more than one caravan on a lot. The Shire can add conditions and can cancel an approval if they're broken. Call the Shire on (08) 9780 5255.

Questions

Common questions

Can I live in a tiny home in Margaret River?

Yes, with Shire approval, on a lot that already has a house, for up to 24 months at a time. It must be up to 50 m² and lived in permanently.

Can I Airbnb a tiny home on wheels in Augusta Margaret River?

Not under this approval. The Shire's tiny home rules are for permanent living, not short-term rental.

Can I live in a caravan while I build in Margaret River?

Yes, in one caravan, once your house has a building permit, on R5 or lower residential lots and rural-type zones.

How long does the Shire take to decide?

If it hasn't approved your application within 63 days, it's taken as refused, and you can apply to the Minister.
