Council guide

City of Busselton tiny home rules

Short answer

The City of Busselton can approve living in one caravan or tiny home on wheels per lot for up to 24 months. It must be up to 12.5 m x 2.5 m x 4.3 m, no more than 50 m² with any structures, on land zoned for a house, with a parking bay.

Last updated 24 November 2026

What the City allows

The City of Busselton can approve living temporarily in a caravan or tiny home on wheels on private land. The approval lasts up to 24 months.

RuleDetails
How manyOne caravan or tiny home on wheels per lot
SizeUp to 12.5 m long, 2.5 m wide and 4.3 m high, including wheels and drawbar
Floor areaUp to 50 m², including the van and any structures that go with it. It's treated like a granny flat
BuiltOff site and towed in. Must be self-contained and liveable
ZoningLand zoned to allow a house
ParkingAt least one bay

Where it can go

It should be set back far enough from neighbours to suit the City, and in residential areas it shouldn't be seen from the street. It must sit within any approved building envelope and follow any bushfire permit for the land. No clearing without a permit.

What to send

  • The City's application form, signed by you and all landowners, with the fee.
  • Why you want to live there, start and end dates, and who will live there.
  • Details of drinking water, wastewater, rubbish and power.
  • A current Certificate of Title and anything registered on it, such as easements.
  • A site plan to scale (1:200 or better) showing boundaries, the van, services, other buildings, driveway and setbacks.
  • A floor plan with windows and floor area.
  • A check of the state's Aboriginal heritage system for your land.

Sheds, carports, rigid annexes and tanks over 5,000 litres need their own planning and building approvals.

After you apply

If the City hasn't approved it within 63 days, it's taken as refused. Approvals name the people living there, have conditions, and may require you to clear the site when they end.

Questions

Common questions

Can I live in a tiny home on wheels in Busselton?

Yes, with City approval, for up to 24 months. One per lot, on land zoned for a house, up to 50 m² including any associated structures.

How big can a tiny home on wheels be in Busselton?

Up to 12.5 m long, 2.5 m wide and 4.3 m high, including the wheels, drawbar and accessories.

How long does Busselton take to decide?

If the City hasn't approved your application within 63 days, it's taken as refused.

Does a tiny home in Busselton need parking?

Yes. At least one parking bay must be available.
