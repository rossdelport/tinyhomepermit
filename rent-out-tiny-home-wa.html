Question answer

Can I rent out a tiny home in WA?

Short answer

A tiny home on wheels is a caravan, so it usually can't be a short-stay rental on a normal house lot without council approval. A fixed tiny home approved as a dwelling or granny flat can be, but it must be on the state STRA Register, and in Perth an unhosted one needs planning approval past 90 nights a year.

Tiny homes on wheels

Councils usually treat a tiny home on wheels as a caravan. Caravans can't be lived in permanently outside a licensed caravan park, and staying on private land beyond a short visit needs council approval. Councils treat paid stays as a land use: the Shire of Bridgetown-Greenbushes says short-term renting of a tiny home needs development approval, plus building and health approvals. Ask your council before you list it.

Fixed tiny homes and granny flats

If your tiny home is approved as a building (for example a granny flat), you can usually rent it out. For stays under 3 months:

  • It must be on the state Short-Term Rental Accommodation (STRA) Register before you advertise or take bookings, and the registration number must be shown in the ad.
  • If you live in the main house, it's hosted and doesn't need planning approval.
  • If nobody lives on site (unhosted) in Perth, you need planning approval once you rent it for more than 90 nights in 12 months.
  • Registration lasts 12 months, and penalties apply for not registering.

Read the City of Kwinana tiny home rules, the Shire of Mundaring rules, or wheels vs fixed.

Questions

Common questions

Can I Airbnb a tiny home on wheels in Perth?

Usually not on a normal house lot. A tiny home on wheels is a caravan, and councils such as Mundaring only allow it in an approved caravan park. Ask your council first.

Do I need to register a tiny home on Airbnb in WA?

If it's approved as a dwelling, such as a granny flat, yes. It must be on the STRA Register before you advertise or take bookings.

Do I need planning approval to rent out a granny flat as a short stay?

Not if you live in the main house (hosted). If unhosted in Perth, only once you rent it for more than 90 nights in 12 months.

Can I rent my tiny home long term?

Some councils allow it. Kwinana allows private renting of a tiny home on wheels for more than 3 months with a management plan, as part of its approval.
