Applying

Applying to your council for a tiny home

Short answer

For a tiny home on wheels, you apply to your local government with the landowner's consent, a site plan, details of the tiny home and how water, toilet, shower, waste and power are handled. Approvals can run for up to 24 months and be renewed.

Last updated 29 October 2026

What goes in a tiny home on wheels application

  • The application form for your local government, under the caravan and camping regulations.
  • Landowner consent, if you don't own the land.
  • A site plan showing where the tiny home will sit, its distance from boundaries and buildings, and access.
  • Details of the tiny home: size, photos, and its registration if it is towed on roads.
  • Services: drinking water, toilet and shower, how grey water and sewage are handled, power and gas.
  • How long you want the approval for, up to 24 months.
  • Fees, which vary between councils.

How long it takes

It varies between councils. Simple applications on a lot with a house are often decided in a few weeks. Applications needing the Minister's approval, or on lots without services, take longer.

Tips that help an application

  • Show the tiny home has its own toilet and shower, or easy access to the main house's.
  • Explain how waste water is handled, with plumber's details if it connects to sewer.
  • Keep it clear of boundaries and driveways, and out of the front yard.
  • Talk to the neighbours early. It costs nothing and avoids complaints later.

Renewing your approval

Before your approval runs out, you can apply to extend it for a further period. We keep a note of your expiry date and can handle the renewal.

Questions

Common questions

Can my council refuse a tiny home?

Yes. Councils weigh up health, safety and amenity. Refusals often come down to missing facilities, waste water or fire safety, which a good application addresses up front.

Is there a fee?

Councils charge an application fee, which varies. We include it in our fixed quote so you know the total.
