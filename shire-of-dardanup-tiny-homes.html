Shire of Dardanup tiny home rules

Short answer

In the Shire of Dardanup, a tiny home on wheels counts as a caravan. You can stay up to 5 nights with the owner's OK, or up to 24 months with written approval. A fixed tiny home needs a building permit certified by a private building surveyor.

Fixed tiny homes: building rules

A tiny home not on wheels needs a building permit, and may need planning approval too. In Dardanup, the permit application must be certified by a private building surveyor (a BA1). It must meet the building code and the Shire's health local laws, including:

  • a kitchen, bathroom and laundry,
  • ceilings at least 2.4 m in living rooms and bedrooms, and 2.1 m in bathrooms, toilets, laundries and kitchens,
  • connection to sewer or an approved on-site wastewater system,
  • power, water and hot water, with compliance for electrical, plumbing and any gas work,
  • smoke alarms, ventilation, light and energy efficiency,
  • termite protection and bushfire construction if in a bushfire prone area, and
  • engineer certification of the structure, including how it's fixed to the ground.

Planning rules

Fixed tiny homes must meet the Shire's Local Planning Scheme No. 9 and its policies, whether used as a house or a granny flat. That covers the R-Codes, setbacks or building envelopes, services and bushfire rules.

Check before you buy. Call the Shire on (08) 9724 0000. See fixed tiny home permits, tiny home on wheels rules and connecting services.

Questions

Common questions

Can I live in a tiny home on wheels in Dardanup?

Yes, for up to 5 nights with the landowner's OK. For longer, up to 24 months, you need written approval from the Shire or the Minister. Permanent living is only allowed in a licensed caravan park.

Does a fixed tiny home need a permit in Dardanup?

Yes. It needs a building permit, certified by a private building surveyor, and it may need planning approval too.

What ceiling height does a tiny home need in Dardanup?

At least 2.4 m in living rooms and bedrooms, and 2.1 m in bathrooms, toilets, laundries and kitchens.

Does a tiny home need a kitchen and bathroom in Dardanup?

Yes. A fixed tiny home must have a kitchen, bathroom and laundry.
